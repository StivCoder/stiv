MY STORY

Hi, I'm Stephen

I'm a Nairobi-based freelance software engineer, graphic designer and business consultant, and a core founder of Vectra Tech. I'm also a second-year BSc Software Engineering student at Kirinyaga University.

I build websites and business tools for small and medium businesses across Kenya, from M-Pesa payments to WhatsApp customer flows. I also train people and teams to use AI in their everyday work, and I work on clear written contracts so every project starts with agreed scope, timeline and payment terms.
